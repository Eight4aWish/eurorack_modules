# Wealth: MOD2 and Melon voices

HAGIWO's MOD2 voices and WGD Modular's Melon voices on **Wealth** (the analogue board Rev B with a
Pico 2 W), behind the **MOD2/Melon panel**. They use the same driver as the Workshop Computer programs
(`src/revb/card/ComputerCard.h`, see [REVB_COMPUTER.md](REVB_COMPUTER.md)). The first is the
**LFSR Snare**.

**Status (2026-11-13): builds, not yet run.** The Rev B boards are on order.

## Why a port, not a shim

The MOD2 and Melon sketches drive the RP2350's hardware directly: the snare, for one, runs its
own ADC and PWM DMA and fills audio buffers on core 1. A header can't redirect that. Each voice
keeps its sound engine as published and gets a new frame around it:

- `ProcessSample()` calls the voice's per-sample renderer at the codec's 47 973.6 Hz.
- Lookup tables built from `SAMPLE_RATE` are rebuilt at the new rate, so pitches and decay times
  stay put.
- The MOD2's output is reproduced in software: 8-bit PWM steps, the two-pole RC filter (JP1), and
  the AC-coupled ×3.06 amplifier (JP2).
- POT3 and the CV jack, which share the original's A2, become Y plus CV in 1.

## The MOD2/Melon panel

The Computer panel's cells and holes, so one wired board runs either family. Blank cells are
left undrilled.

| Row | Left | Middle | Right |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Screen | Encoder (push = BUTTON) | Filter switch |
| 2 | POT1 | POT2 | POT3 |
| 3 | IN1 | CV | — |
| 4 | IN2 | — | — |
| 5 | — | — | OUT |
| 6 | — | — | OUT DC |

| MOD2 | Panel | Rev B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| POT1, POT2, POT3 | Main, X, Y cells | `KnobVal(Main/X/Y)` |
| IN1 (GPIO7), IN2 (GPIO0) | Pulse in 1, 2 | `PulseIn1`, `PulseIn2` |
| CV (on A2, with POT3) | CV in 1 | added to Y: 0 to +5 V spans the pot |
| BUTTON (GPIO6) | the encoder's push | `PushPressed()` |
| JP1, the RC filter's cutoff | the filter switch, in Z's cell and wired as Z | up 15.9 kHz, down 5.0 kHz |
| JP2, the output coupling | two jacks | OUT is AC-coupled (1.6 Hz), OUT DC is DC-coupled |
| LED (GPIO5), Melon's WS2812 | the screen | LED 0 |

OUT DC is what the MOD2 gives with JP2 closed: 0 to about +10 V, +5 V at rest. It is for the
voices with envelope and LFO modes (`tides`). For a drum, use OUT.

## LFSR Snare

HAGIWO's MOD2 "LFSR Snare Ver1.0" (`modulove/MOD2`, `Firmware/snare/snare.ino`, CC0 1.0): a
retro-game-style one-shot snare from a 15-bit LFSR. Its README header says most of its code was
generated by AI.

| Control | Does |
| --- | --- |
| Main | decay, 15 to 400 ms |
| X | the LFSR's clock, 1 to 32 kHz |
| Y (+ CV in 1) | pitch-envelope amount, 0 to 4 octaves |
| Pulse in 1, the push | trigger |
| Pulse in 2 | high around the trigger: half level |
| Filter switch | up for the snare's bright 15.9 kHz, down for 5.0 kHz |

Changed for the new rate: the click envelope's per-sample coefficient is rescaled from 31.25 kHz,
and the LFSR clock keeps its 125 kHz cap in Hz. The noise is sampled at 48 kHz rather than
31.25 kHz, so its aliasing differs a little from a MOD2's. Compare it with the breadboard MOD2
on the bench once that's built.

```sh
cmake --build build/revb --target snare    # build/revb/apps/snare/snare.uf2
```
